# Public procurement signals — 14 October 2026

RevenueOS Live surfaced four previously unreported public records in its 14 October scan. The underlying notices were published on **30 September 2026**; this is therefore a monitoring update, not a claim that the procurements were first published today.

## Screening note

- Source: public notices in the EU's official Tenders Electronic Daily (TED) database, cross-checked against the official TED Search API on 14 October 2026.
- RevenueOS screening score for each record: **62/100 fit** and **56% confidence**.
- Those numbers are deterministic triage scores, not win probabilities or verified buying intent.
- No employer, customer, or private lead information is used.

## 1. Finland — ICT expert-services framework, Lot 12

**Facts**

- Buyer: Metsähallitus and Metsähallitus Metsätalous Oy.
- Procedure: open competition for Lot 12, basic ICT expert services, within a 14-lot framework.
- The notice says the buyer intends to select up to three suppliers for this lot.
- Estimated lot value: **€2,000,000**.
- Tender deadline: **9 November 2026**.
- CPV codes include 72000000, 72100000 and 72266000.
- Official notice: [TED 670727-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/670727-2026)

**Inference**

This is the strongest near-term commercial signal in the set because it is an open procedure with a stated value and deadline. The description is broad, however; a supplier should inspect the tender documents, qualification requirements, delivery language and framework call-off mechanics before treating it as a qualified opportunity.

## 2. Germany — GeoOpenSource framework

**Facts**

- Buyer: Dataport AöR.
- Procedure: open competition covering eight lots.
- The lots include consulting, operations advice and development around deegree.pro, MapServer/deegree community, GeoServer cloud, LDProxy, XPlanBox, Masterportal, QGIS, and maintenance of Civitas Core.
- Tender deadline: **3 November 2026**.
- CPV code: 72000000.
- Official notice: [TED 670742-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/670742-2026)

**Inference**

This is a specific public-sector open-source geospatial services signal rather than a general software purchase. It may fit specialist delivery partners with the named-stack credentials; the German-language notice and lot-specific requirements are likely material filters.

## 4. Spain — application quality-assurance consulting result

**Facts**

- Buyer: Dirección General de Infraestructuras Educativas.
- Notice type: contract-award/result notice from a restricted procedure.
- Scope: consulting services for quality assurance of applications and software developments managed by the education and innovation IT subdirectorate.
- CPV codes include 72000000 and 72200000.

**Inference**

This is not a live opportunity. It is useful as evidence of public-sector demand for software-quality assurance and as a benchmark for future related procurements, but it should be excluded from open-opportunity counts.

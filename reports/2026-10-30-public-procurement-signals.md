## Qualified live signal

### Almería seeks a SaaS platform for centrally managed public websites

#### Verified facts

- **Buyer:** Junta de Gobierno de la Diputación Provincial de Almería, Spain.
- **Requirement:** a subscription to a technology platform for the central management of portals and websites across the provincial communications network, delivered as SaaS with hosting and maintenance.
- **Procedure:** open competition for software packages and information systems.
- **Estimated contract value:** **€379,338.83 excluding VAT**.
- **Base procurement budget:** **€326,446.27**.
- **Tender deadline:** **13 November 2026 at 23:59**.
- **Published:** 16 October 2026.
- **Official notice:** [TED 715143-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/715143-2026).
- **Public tender summary:** [EU Public Data record](https://eupublicdata.eu/data/tenders/715143-2026/).

#### Qualification view

This is a genuine external SaaS procurement with a named public buyer, a future deadline and a defined subscription, hosting and maintenance scope. It is closer to digital-experience and web-content infrastructure than to industrial operations software. A plausible supplier should study the Spanish procurement documents, confirm local and EU tender eligibility, validate hosting and security requirements, and determine whether the platform must be an existing product or may include implementation and customisation.

RevenueOS assigned a screening score of 62/100 and confidence of 56%. Those figures are deterministic demo triage aids, not buyer facts or purchase probabilities.

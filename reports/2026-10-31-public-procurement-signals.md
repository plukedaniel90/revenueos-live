# Public procurement signals — 2026-10-31

RevenueOS surfaced four European records for review on 31 October 2026. Two are verified open software competitions. One is a contract-award result and therefore a market signal rather than a live pursuit. The remaining Croatian record is excluded because the public evidence available in this review does not support treating it as an open opportunity.

This report uses public facts only. The qualification comments are commercial hypotheses, not claims about buyer intent, supplier eligibility, available budget or probability of award.

## Qualified live signal — urgent

### Germany: BSI seeks protection-profile updates, evaluation and a standardised API

#### Verified facts

- **Buyer:** Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik (BSI), Bonn, Germany.
- **Project:** Projekt 1025, “CSP Update, Evaluierung, Rezertifizierung und API (CUERA)”.
- **Scope:** IT consulting and software-development services covering updates to CSP and CSP-Light protection profiles, evaluation against the EU Cybersecurity Certification Scheme, and a standardised interface description.
- **Lots:** three: profile update and modularisation; evaluation; standardised API description.
- **Procedure:** open, above the EU threshold.
- **Tender deadline:** **3 November 2026 at 10:00 CET**.
- **Notice update published:** **19 October 2026**.
- **Published value:** not stated in the notice summary reviewed.
- **Official notice:** [TED 718795-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/718795-2026).
- **German public-procurement mirror:** [Hofmann notice record](https://ausschreibungen.icc-hofmann.de/cgi-bin/docorder?FM_ND=2026101900482244979).

#### Qualification view

This is a genuine external software and cybersecurity-services competition with a named federal buyer and a future deadline. The scope is specialised and likely requires German-language bidding, EU procurement eligibility, recognised security-evaluation expertise and close reading of the three lot specifications. The short remaining window makes this suitable only for an already-qualified supplier or partner. RevenueOS’s 62/100 screening score and 56% confidence are demo triage aids, not purchase probabilities.

## Qualified live signal

### Italy: ASST Bergamo Ovest seeks support and evolution of hospital reception systems

#### Verified facts

- **Buyer:** ASST di Bergamo Ovest, Treviglio, Italy.
- **Requirement:** technical assistance, corrective maintenance and evolutionary maintenance for the CUP, emergency-department and ADT reception systems installed at the health authority.
- **Procedure:** open competition.
- **Estimated value:** **€1,667,417 excluding VAT**.
- **Contract duration:** **36 months**.
- **Tender deadline:** **18 November 2026 at 12:00 CET**.
- **Published:** **19 October 2026**.
- **Main classification:** CPV 72250000, system and support services; the notice also includes helpdesk and support services.
- **Official notice:** [TED 718813-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/718813-2026).
- **Public TED-data record:** [EU Tenders record](https://eutenders.initask.com/notice/718813-2026).

#### Qualification view

This is a live, budgeted health-system support procurement. It appears to concern an installed system and may therefore favour the incumbent or suppliers with access to the existing application, local healthcare integration knowledge and Italian-language delivery capability. The procurement documents must confirm intellectual-property access, interoperability requirements, eligibility and whether equivalent third-party support is feasible. It is a valid market signal even if Northstar would ultimately decide not to bid.

- **Estimated value:** **€560,000 excluding VAT**.
- **Term:** 37 months.
- **Tender deadline:** **11 November 2026 at 23:59 WET**.
- **Language:** Portuguese.
- **Published:** 14 October 2026 in OJ S 199/2026.

A potential supplier should first test Portuguese-language delivery, local procurement eligibility, required staffing certifications, and whether the buyer permits the proposed delivery model.

## 2. Children's Health Ireland seeks a hospital logistics software system

### Verified facts

- **Buyer:** Children's Health Ireland (CHI).
- **Requirement:** one supplier to provide, implement, and support a Dock Door Management, Messaging and Final Mile Delivery System for the new children's hospital.
- **Scope described by the buyer:** loading dock scheduling, delivery management and tracking, automated identification and routing of goods, returns, integration with automated guided vehicle workflows, and final-mile communications to hospital users.
- **Procedure:** open competition for services.
- **Published estimate:** **€1 excluding VAT**. This appears to be a placeholder and should not be treated as a usable budget signal.
- **Expected duration:** 9 years.
- **Tender deadline:** **12 November 2026 at 17:00 CET**.
- **Clarification deadline:** 30 October 2026 at 17:00 CET.
- **Published:** 14 October 2026 in OJ S 199/2026.
- **Official notice:** [TED 707513-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/707513-2026)

### Qualification view

This is the strongest functional match in the current set because the notice describes an operational logistics system, implementation work, hardware installation, software maintenance, and workflow integration. The long stated duration could make support capability material. Commercial qualification still depends on the procurement documents, integration requirements, hospital deployment constraints, eligibility, and the buyer's evaluation model. The published €1 estimate supplies no credible budget information.

The short remaining window, Czech or Slovak submission requirement, restricted procurement documents, security-related provisions, and local public procurement rules are material qualification gates.

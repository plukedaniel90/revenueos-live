## 1. Portugal — maritime nuclear-fission ship design and safety research

**Facts**

- Buyer: European Maritime Safety Agency.
- Procedure: open competition.
- Scope: ship design and safety assessment of nuclear-fission technologies for maritime applications.
- The notice describes research into zero-emission, long-endurance propulsion for large vessels and work relevant to the International Maritime Organization's review of rules for nuclear-powered ships.
- Estimated value: **€1,000,000**.
- Tender deadline: **7 December 2026**.
- CPV codes include 73112000 and 73210000.
- Official notice: [TED 674253-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/674253-2026)

**Inference**

This is a genuine and unusually specific research-services opportunity, but it is not a general digital-transformation tender. A credible bidder would likely need demonstrable maritime architecture, nuclear-safety, regulatory and advanced-engineering expertise, potentially through a specialist consortium. It should not enter an active pipeline merely because the automated screen matched “digital transformation.”

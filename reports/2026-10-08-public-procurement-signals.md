# RevenueOS Public Procurement Signals — 8 October 2026

## Scope and qualification

RevenueOS surfaced eight European public-procurement records in its live feed on 8 October 2026. Each underlying TED notice is dated **24 September 2026**. This report therefore treats them as **newly observed monitoring signals**, not as procurements newly published today.

All information below comes from public TED records exposed by RevenueOS. No employer, customer, private-lead or personal information is included.

Important limitations:

- RevenueOS fit scores are deterministic screening indicators, not probabilities of purchase or win.
- Published contract values are not disclosed in the monitored records.
- Five notices do not expose a response deadline in the feed; the official notice must be checked before action.
- Several records lack an English lot description in the feed, so their technical scope and buyer identity remain unverified here.
- A capability-label match does not establish budget, eligibility, decision-maker access or product fit.

## Bottom line

This batch contains genuine public demand, but no record is ready to call a qualified sales opportunity from the feed alone. The strongest verification targets are the Clean Hydrogen managed-IT framework and Portugal’s health Data Lake and disaster-recovery procurements. The Inven2 notice deserves technical review because its high automated score may overstate commercial fit. All eight require the official specification, buyer eligibility and current deadline to be checked before outreach.

- **Tender deadline:** **5 November 2026 at 09:00 UTC**.
- **Submission language:** Finnish.
- **Award model for the core lot:** 50 points for price and 50 points for quality.
- **Published value:** not stated in the notice.
- **Notice status:** change notice adding missing attachments and correcting or clarifying procurement documents.
- **Published:** 15 October 2026 in OJ S 200/2026.

The notice does not establish budget, technical architecture, integration scope, or fit for a specific vendor. The near deadline, Finnish-only submission, already-closed clarification window, and need to study the amended documents are material qualification gates.

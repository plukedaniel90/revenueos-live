# Public procurement signals — 17 October 2026

RevenueOS Live surfaced four previously unreported public records in its 17 October scan. The underlying notices were published on **5 October 2026**. Official TED data shows one live open competition, one supplier-qualification system, one prior-information/planning notice and one contract-result notice. This report separates actionable procurement, early market intelligence and closed business.

## Screening note

- Source: public records in the EU's official Tenders Electronic Daily (TED) database, cross-checked against the official TED Search API on 17 October 2026.
- RevenueOS assigned each record **62/100 fit** and **56% confidence**.
- These are deterministic triage scores, not win probabilities, supplier eligibility findings or verified buying intent.
- Translations below are concise working translations of the official Czech, Polish and Italian notice text.
- No employer, customer or private lead information is used.

## 1. European Commission — support for the AI Observatory

**Facts**

- Buyer: European Commission, DG CNECT — Communications Networks, Content and Technology.
- Notice type: live standard competition under an open procedure.
- Scope: establish and support an AI Observatory with robust indicators for AI's effects in strategic sectors, the labour market and the wider economy, and support sectoral discussions.
- Estimated value: **€2.5 million**.
- Tender deadline: **3 November 2026**.
- CPV code: 73200000, research and development consultancy services.
- Official notice: [TED 681546-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/681546-2026)

**Inference**

This is the strongest current signal in the set. It appears to require economic and labour-market measurement, indicator design, policy research and stakeholder facilitation rather than a conventional software implementation. A credible bid would probably need EU-policy experience and a research-led consortium. Review the full procurement documents before treating the indexed value, work split or eligibility as settled.

## 2. Italy — Enel hardware-maintenance qualification system

**Facts**

- Buyer: Enel SpA.
- Notice type: qualification-system notice, version 2; it is not a discrete contract award.
- Scope: create a list of qualified companies for the hardware-maintenance category MWIT03.
- The notice directs suppliers to Enel's Global Procurement portal for the underlying qualification requirements.
- CPV code: 72611000, technical computer support services.
- No indexed contract value or single tender deadline is stated in the TED search record.
- Official notice: [TED 681593-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/681593-2026)

**Inference**

This may be an actionable supplier-registration route for firms with hardware-maintenance delivery capacity in Enel's markets, but it is broader and earlier than a tender. RevenueOS should track qualification-system notices separately from bid-ready opportunities and verify the portal requirements before recommending outreach.

## 3. Czechia — planned HCL Domino subscription transition

**Facts**

- Buyer: Czech Ministry of Justice.
- Notice type: prior-information/planning notice, not a current contract award.
- Scope: support for **4,200 HCL Domino Complete Collaboration licences** and transition to a subscription licensing model.
- The TED record identifies an open procedure and CPV code 72261000, software support services.
- The indexed zero value does not establish that the future procurement has no commercial value; no usable estimated value is disclosed in the search record.
- Official notice: [TED 681515-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/681515-2026)

**Inference**

This is credible advance market intelligence for HCL licensing, migration and support specialists. It is not yet evidence of a bid-ready deadline. Track the follow-on contract notice and avoid placing the record in the active pipeline until procurement documents and dates are published.

## 4. Poland — Omnivise T3000 service contract result

**Facts**

- Buyer: Zakład Unieszkodliwiania Odpadów Sp. z o.o., the Szczecin waste-treatment facility.
- Notice type: contract-award/result notice following a negotiated procedure without a prior call for competition.
- Scope: service support for the facility's DCS Omnivise T3000 system.
- Named winner: Siemens Energy Sp. z o.o.
- CPV code: 72000000, IT services.
- Official notice: [TED 681584-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/681584-2026)

**Inference**

This is closed business, not an open opportunity. It provides limited market intelligence about the installed control-system environment and incumbent supplier. It should be excluded from active-opportunity counts, and its broad IT-services CPV code should not by itself trigger a digital-transformation recommendation.

## Practical follow-up

1. Prioritise a document-level qualification of the AI Observatory competition before the 3 November deadline, focusing on consortium needs, evaluation criteria and required evidence.
2. Route the Enel notice to supplier-registration review rather than tender pursuit.
3. Monitor the Czech planning notice for a later contract notice and concrete deadline.
4. Remove the Polish result from active pipeline totals.
5. Improve RevenueOS labelling by classifying notice stage before scoring fit: competition, qualification system, planning and result should not share the same action label.

# Public procurement signals — 16 October 2026

RevenueOS Live surfaced three previously unreported public records in its 16 October scan. The underlying notices were published on **2 October 2026**. Official TED data shows that only one is a live competition; the other two are contract-result notices. This report therefore separates actionable procurement from market intelligence.

## Screening note

- Source: public records in the EU's official Tenders Electronic Daily (TED) database, cross-checked against the official TED Search API on 16 October 2026.
- RevenueOS assigned screening scores of **62–63/100 fit** and **56–64% confidence**.
- These are deterministic triage scores, not win probabilities, supplier eligibility findings or verified buying intent.
- No employer, customer or private lead information is used.

## 1. Poland — IBM Cognos Planning Analytics support and licences

**Facts**

- Buyer: Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego.
- Notice type: contract-award/result notice, not a live tender.
- Scope: three years of manufacturer technical support for existing IBM Cognos Planning Analytics licences, plus additional Cognos licences or equivalents and support through 31 December 2029.
- Procedure recorded as open.
- CPV codes include 48000000 and 72267000.
- Official notice: [TED 677989-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/677989-2026)

**Inference**

This is useful evidence of continuing public-sector demand for enterprise planning analytics, licensing and support. It should not be counted as an open opportunity. The detailed Polish result may still help identify incumbency, contract structure and likely renewal patterns.

## 2. Spain — operational logistics support for defence personnel

**Facts**

- Buyer: Jefatura de Asuntos Económicos del Estado Mayor de la Defensa.
- Notice type: live open competition.
- Stated scope: integrated logistical support for MOPS/EMAD personnel in an area of operations.
- Tender deadline: **19 October 2026**.
- CPV code: 72253000.
- The official record is in Spanish and does not disclose an indexed value.
- Official notice: [TED 677856-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/677856-2026)

**Inference**

Despite the CPV classification and RevenueOS “digital transformation” label, the title and description indicate operational personnel logistics rather than a conventional technology helpdesk opportunity. The deadline is also imminent. Treat this as a likely automated-screen false positive unless the full tender documents reveal a material IT service component and achievable eligibility.

## 3. Latvia — Riga Castle history exhibition delivery

**Facts**

- Buyer: Latvian National History Museum.
- Notice type: contract-award/result notice, not a live tender.
- Scope: manufacture, delivery and installation of the “History of Riga Castle” exhibition.
- The classification set spans museum services, structures, artistic services, exhibition fittings, video production and software-development services.
- Official notice: [TED 677963-2026](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/677963-2026)

**Inference**

The software-related classification explains why the automated screen surfaced the record, but the procurement is primarily an exhibition-production engagement. It is relevant only to multidisciplinary museum-experience suppliers and should be excluded from general digital-transformation opportunity counts.

## Practical follow-up

1. Exclude the Polish and Latvian result notices from active-pipeline totals while retaining them as market intelligence.
2. Review the complete Spanish tender only if defence operational-logistics delivery is within scope; otherwise close it as a false positive.
3. Improve RevenueOS filtering by penalising contract-result notices and requiring stronger textual evidence before labelling a record “digital transformation.”
